# Mempool Basics

## What is a Mempool?

A mempool is a temporary area where pending blockchain transactions wait before being included in a block.

## Why It Matters

- Shows pending transactions
- Helps estimate network activity
- Affects transaction timing
- Can influence gas fee decisions

## Base

Transactions submitted on Base may spend a short time waiting before they are processed and included in the network state.

## Learning Goal

Understand what happens to a blockchain transaction between submission and confirmation.
