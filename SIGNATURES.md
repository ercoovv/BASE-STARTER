# Digital Signatures

## What is a Digital Signature?

A digital signature proves that a blockchain action was approved by the owner of a wallet.

## Common Uses

- Signing transactions
- Signing messages
- Approving smart contract interactions
- Verifying wallet ownership
- Authorizing onchain actions

## Why It Matters

Digital signatures help secure blockchain activity without exposing private keys.

## Learning Goal

Understand how wallets authorize actions on Base and other Ethereum-compatible networks.
